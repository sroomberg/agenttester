## Stay in Scope

Only change what the task requires. Do not refactor, rename, reformat, or reorganize code that isn't directly involved in the change you are making — even if you notice something that could be improved. Unrelated edits make diffs harder to review and can introduce unexpected regressions.

Do not clean up surrounding code, adjust indentation, reorder imports, or fix style issues outside the files you need to touch. If you notice a bug or problem unrelated to the task, note it in your response rather than fixing it silently.

When in doubt, do less. A focused, minimal diff is easier to evaluate than a broad one.
